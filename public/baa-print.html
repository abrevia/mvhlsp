1. Neutral Linguistic Conduit & Telecommunications Scope

Business Associate provides specialized real-time oral and video interpretation (OPI/VRI). In accordance with the HHS HIPAA Privacy Rule (45 CFR § 164.502) and the Neutral Conduit Doctrine, transmission of transient verbal/visual Protected Health Information (PHI) occurs with Zero-Data-Trace (ZDT). No audio, video, or clinical communication is recorded, stored, mined, or retained.

2. Safeguards and Regulatory Obligations

Business Associate maintains administrative, physical, and technical safeguards in compliance with 45 CFR § 164.308, § 164.310, and § 164.312, ensuring all interpreters are bound by statutory confidentiality and HIPAA omnibus mandates.
