Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

1. Who we are

InterpretLive MVHLSP provides on-demand overflow medical interpretation (over-the-phone and video remote interpreting) to healthcare organizations. This website, mvhlsp.site, is our marketing and activation site.

2. Health information during an interpretation

Interpretation sessions are delivered as a neutral linguistic conduit over encrypted real-time audio and video. Clinical conversation is not recorded, stored, transcribed, or mined on our systems. Where protected health information (PHI) is involved, our relationship with a healthcare provider is governed by a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) executed before the first encounter.

3. Information collected on this website

If you use a contact or BAA request form, the details you enter (clinic name, your name, work email, optional phone number, request type) are used only to respond to your request and prepare your agreement. We do not sell this information. Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes the standard technical data needed to serve the page securely.

4. Email contact

If you email us, we keep the message and your reply address so we can answer and maintain the business relationship. Please do not send patient-identifying details by email.

5. Cookies and advertising

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party marketing pixels. Any storage used is limited to what is required for the page to function and to keep our hosting secure.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the business contact information you have sent us at any time. Requests that conflict with a legal or contractual retention obligation will be explained in our reply.

7. Contact

Privacy questions and data requests: info@mvhlsp.site.

Terms of Service & Neutral Conduit Doctrine

1. Neutral Linguistic Conduit Doctrine

MVHLSP and its independent interpreting collective operate strictly as verbatim linguistic transmission conduits under Section 1557 ACA and HIPAA Privacy standards. Interpreters do not practice medicine, render clinical diagnoses, or evaluate medical necessity. All medical decisions and care remain under the non-delegable authority of the attending licensed healthcare provider.

2. Zero-Data-Trace (ZDT) Operational Architecture

Encounters facilitated via MVHLSP use encrypted real-time telecommunications (SIP-TLS, SRTP, and WebRTC). In accordance with HHS HIPAA conduit exceptions, transient spoken and visual protected health information (PHI) is never permanently stored, recorded, transcribed, or retained on intermediary servers.

3. Transparent Per-Second Metering

Services are rendered on-demand at the per-minute rate published in the Pricing section of this site, billed to the second. There are zero monthly minimum commitments, zero retainer fees, and zero locked contracts. Metering begins upon active interpreter connection and concludes immediately upon session disconnection.

4. Professional Ethics & Standards

All interpreters operate in strict alignment with the National Council on Interpreting in Health Care (NCIHC) National Code of Ethics and ASTM F2089 standards for healthcare interpreting.
