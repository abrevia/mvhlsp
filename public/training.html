40-Hour Medical Interpreter Training

Our own training program for MVHLSP roster interpreters, built in-house and free of charge. It follows industry standards for medical interpreter training and is delivered in two documents totaling 40 hours.

Document A

28 h

Universal core: role, ethics & NCIHC standards, HIPAA/PHI & BAA, ASTM F2089 principles, protocols & OPI/VRI, cultural competence, professional practice.

Document B

12 h

Language-specific module: 500+ term medical glossary with usage notes, a patient-speech traps section, case studies, practical exercises and self-assessment.

Final exam

100 Q

Passing score 80/100. On passing, MVHLSP issues a Certificate of Completion with a unique certificate ID.

Available now: English ↔ Spanish

The pilot edition (version 1.1) is published for the English–Spanish pair. Additional language pairs are in preparation and will be added to this page.

The instructor answer key is not published: it is held by MVHLSP for scoring. To submit a completed exam and receive the Certificate of Completion, write to info@mvhlsp.site.

How the program works

  1. Read Document A (universal core) and Document B (your language pair). Together they total 40 hours.
  2. Take the final examination without notes or outside help. Passing score: 80 out of 100.
  3. Send your completed answer sheet to info@mvhlsp.site. MVHLSP scores it and issues your Certificate of Completion by email, with a unique certificate ID.

The Certificate of Completion records that you successfully completed MVHLSP’s proprietary 40-hour course, designed following industry standards. It is training, not a professional certification or accreditation. MVHLSP is not affiliated with CCHI, NBCMI or ASTM.
