Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 November 2026

1. Who we are

InterpretLive MVHLSP ("MVHLSP", "we") provides on-demand overflow medical interpretation (over-the-phone and video remote interpreting) to healthcare organizations in the United States. This website, mvhlsp.site, is our marketing and activation site.

2. Health information during an interpretation

Interpretation sessions are delivered as a neutral linguistic conduit over encrypted real-time audio and video. Clinical conversation is not recorded, stored, transcribed, or mined on our systems. Where protected health information (PHI) is involved, our relationship with a healthcare provider is governed by a Business Associate Agreement (BAA) executed before the first encounter.

3. Information collected on this website

If you use a contact or BAA request form on this site, the details you enter (clinic name, your name, work email, optional phone number, request type) are used only to respond to your request and to prepare your agreement. We do not sell this information.

Like most websites, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) processes standard technical data needed to serve the page securely, such as the requested URL, approximate region, and browser type.

4. Email contact

If you email us, we keep the message and your reply address so we can answer and maintain the business relationship. Please do not send patient-identifying details by email.

5. Cookies and advertising

This site does not use advertising cookies or third-party marketing pixels. Any storage used is limited to what is required for the page to function and to keep our hosting secure.

6. Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete the business contact information you have sent us at any time by writing to the address below. Requests that conflict with a legal or contractual retention obligation will be explained in our reply.
